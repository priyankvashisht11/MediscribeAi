───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
WHAT MEDISCRIBE ACTUALLY DOES (for HeyGen context accuracy):
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

MediScribe is a live AI medical scribe. The doctor opens the app,
starts a session, and speaks naturally with their patient.
MediScribe records and transcribes in real time using Deepgram
Nova-3 — a model trained on medical vocabulary, tuned for Indian
English accents. It automatically separates the doctor's voice
from the patient's voice (speaker diarization) so the transcript
is correctly attributed.

When the session ends, the AI (Perplexity sonar-reasoning-pro —
a medical reasoning model, not a generic chatbot) reads the full
transcript and generates a complete SOAP note:
  - Chief Complaint
  - Subjective (patient history, HPI)
  - Objective (vitals, exam findings)
  - Assessment (diagnosis, differentials)
  - Plan (medications, investigations, follow-up)
  - ICD-10 Codes (for billing and insurance)

The doctor reviews, edits inline if needed, and clicks Finalize.

Three documents are then available for download:

1. SOAP NOTE PDF — Full structured clinical note with all sections,
   ICD-10 codes, doctor name, patient name, age, date, duration,
   AI confidence score. Clinic-branded. Print-ready.

2. PRESCRIPTION PDF — Structured Rx with each medication listed:
   drug name, dose, frequency, duration, special instructions.
   Investigations advised. Follow-up date. Doctor signature line.
   Ready to hand to patient or send to pharmacy.

3. PATIENT INSTRUCTIONS PDF — Plain-English visit summary:
   "What Was Found Today" (AI simplifies clinical language),
   "Your Medications" with instructions,
   "Tests To Get Done",
   "Come Back Immediately If:" (AI-extracted warning signs in
   plain language the patient can understand),
   Follow-up instructions.
   Designed for the patient to take home.

All infrastructure runs in India (Mumbai). No audio is stored after
transcription. The app runs in any browser on any device — no
installation, no app download, no IT setup required.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
WHY DOCTORS DESPERATELY WANT THIS:
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

A busy Indian GP sees 40–80 patients per OPD session.
Each note takes 3–5 minutes to write by hand.
That's 2–4 hours of documentation per day — unpaid, unrecognized,
and done at the cost of attention to actual patients.

Common complaints from Indian doctors:
- "I can't look at my patient because I'm writing"
- "My notes are incomplete because I rush them"
- "I forget details from the first patient by the time I see the tenth"
- "Prescriptions are illegible — patients go to wrong pharmacy"
- "Insurance rejections because ICD codes were wrong or missing"
- "I go home exhausted from paperwork, not from medicine"

MediScribe solves all of this. The doctor never holds a pen during
consultation. Full attention stays on the patient. The note is more
complete than anything hand-written because it's based on the full
conversation, not memory. ICD codes are auto-suggested. Prescription
is typed and structured. Patient leaves with a clear instruction sheet.

ROI: At ₹500/hour saved, MediScribe pays for itself in under 3 days.
